The Experience

Barefoot luxury,
the Kamala way.

Smile sits right on the sand of Kamala Beach, where the Andaman turns gold every evening. Sink into a beanbag with your toes in the sand, or settle at a table by the water.

Our kitchen lives around the charcoal grill: chicken marinated for the better part of a day, lobster and tiger prawns from the coals, and the great classics of Thai cooking — all made to order, all day long.

  • Toes in the sand

    Beanbags and beachfront tables, metres from the water.

  • The charcoal grill

    Our signature chicken, lobster and prawns, kissed by the coals.

  • Golden hour

    Facing west over Kamala Bay — one of Phuket's finest sunsets.

  • Weekend fire show

    Every Friday & Saturday, 21:00 — 21:30, on the beach.
