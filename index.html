Our Story

A journey of taste
& excellence

“I like your smile. No matter the joy or the pain, your smile makes the whole world bright again.”

From this inspiration, Smile was born. In the year 2000, on the golden sands of Kamala Beach, a small beverage kiosk stood by the sea — a place where laughter was shared, friendships began, and happiness was served with every drink.

That tiny wooden kiosk, built with heart and sincerity, grew into Smile Restaurant — yet its soul has never faded. Our heart still beats with the same local fishermen's spirit, the same warmth, and the same joy of serving people like family.

Smile — where every meal begins with a smile, and ends with one too.
